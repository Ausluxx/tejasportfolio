NSE Momentum
+ Quality

Markets / Factor Investing · Python
Live dashboard → GitHub →

Do momentum and quality still earn their keep on Indian large-caps once you account for real trading costs?

What I built

A long-only strategy that ranks NSE stocks on combined momentum and quality signals — weighting the two by their information coefficient rather than guessing — builds the portfolio with a quadratic-programming optimizer under real constraints, and tests it walk-forward with transaction costs, all in an interactive dashboard.

The finance behind it

Factor investing, signal combination, portfolio construction under constraints, and why out-of-sample testing and costs are what separate a real edge from a curve-fit.

Fama-French
Factor Engine

Quantitative Finance · Python
Live dashboard → GitHub →

What actually drives returns beyond the market — and how do you report it without overstating the case?

What I built

Constructed Fama-French SMB and HML factors from size/value corner portfolios, ran factor regressions with Newey-West (HAC) standard errors, and layered EWMA volatility, rolling-beta tracking and a bias-diagnostics module — survivorship, look-ahead, sample attrition — into a cached Streamlit dashboard.

The finance behind it

What drives cross-sectional returns, why a single market beta isn't enough, and the statistical discipline that keeps factor results honest.

06 / 09

RemitRight

Fintech · Course: FinTech, SNU
Write-up →

People sending money abroad rarely know if they're getting a fair rate. Can a spread tell them?

What I built

A USD/INR spread-intelligence tool that scores today's spread against a 30-day rolling baseline with z-score modelling on RBI DBIE data (GREEN / AMBER / RED), a structural risk calendar for predictable surges — month-end, FOMC, pre-Diwali — and an all-in cost comparator that includes the correspondent-bank charges most apps quietly leave out.

The finance behind it

Statistical baselines and mean reversion, how macro events move FX spreads, and why the true cost of a transfer is bigger than the headline rate.
