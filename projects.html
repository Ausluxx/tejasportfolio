01 / 09

NSE Momentum + Quality

Markets / Factor Investing · Python

Live dashboard → GitHub →

Do momentum and quality still earn their keep on Indian large-caps once you pay real trading costs?

What I built

A long-only strategy that ranks NSE stocks on combined momentum and quality signals, weighting the two by their information coefficient rather than by gut feel. Portfolios are built with a quadratic-programming optimiser under real-world constraints, then tested walk-forward with transaction costs — all explorable in an interactive dashboard.

The finance behind it

Factor investing, signal combination, and portfolio construction under constraints. The part that actually matters: out-of-sample testing and costs are what separate a real edge from a curve-fit.

02 / 09

Fama-French Factor Engine

Quantitative Finance · Python

Live dashboard → GitHub →

What actually drives returns beyond the market — and how do you report it without overstating the case?

What I built

Constructed Fama-French SMB and HML factors from size/value corner portfolios, ran factor regressions with Newey-West (HAC) standard errors, then layered EWMA volatility, rolling-beta tracking and a bias-diagnostics module — survivorship, look-ahead, sample attrition — into a cached Streamlit dashboard.

The finance behind it

What drives cross-sectional returns, why a single market beta was never enough, and the statistical discipline that keeps a factor result honest instead of merely impressive.

05 / 09

Matiks Retention Case

Product / Research Case

Read the case →
Top 3.3% of 9,172 entrants

Which feature next — or is that entirely the wrong question?

What I built

Built the case hypothesis on a hand-assembled dataset of 947 scraped app-store reviews and 130 community messages, structured into a diagnosis of user drop-off, and reframed the brief from “which feature next” to “nothing a match produces compounds.” Delivered as a 24-slide deck with user journey, competitor analysis and a 50-player k-factor gate before any spend.

Why it mattered

It placed in the top 3.3% of 9,172 registrants — a reminder that the sharpest move is usually reframing the question rather than answering the one you were handed.

06 / 09

RemitRight

Fintech / FX · Course: FinTech, SNU

Write-up →

People sending money home rarely know if the rate is fair. Can a spread simply tell them?

What I built

A USD/INR spread-intelligence tool that scores today’s spread against a 30-day rolling baseline using z-score modelling on RBI DBIE data (green / amber / red), a structural risk calendar for the predictable surges — month-end, FOMC, pre-Diwali — and an all-in cost comparator that includes the correspondent-bank charges most apps quietly omit.

The finance behind it

Statistical baselines and mean reversion, how macro events move FX spreads, and why the true cost of a transfer is always bigger than the headline rate.
