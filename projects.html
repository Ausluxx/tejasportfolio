NSE Momentum + Quality

Factor investing · Python · Streamlit

Live dashboard GitHub

Do momentum and quality work on Indian large-caps after trading costs?

Approach
Rank Nifty 50 stocks on momentum and quality, weighting the two signals by their measured information coefficients (IC²) over a 36-month training window.
What I built
A long-only strategy using a quadratic-programming optimiser with position limits, tested walk-forward with transaction costs, plus an interactive dashboard to compare turnover settings.

Result
Out of sample from February 2022 to June 2026, net of costs, the chosen setting (λ = 0.05) compounded to 1.81× against 1.38× for the index. Best year +34.4% (2023), worst year (6.0%) (2026).
Turnover
The highest-turnover version had the best gross alpha but paid the most in costs, so it didn’t win net; the moderate setting did. Momentum’s measured IC was borderline (−0.0018) against quality’s +0.0064, so the blend leaned 58% quality.

Backtest results are hypothetical and net of modelled transaction costs.

Fama-French Factor Engine

Quantitative finance · Python · Streamlit

Live dashboard GitHub

What explains Indian equity returns beyond the market?

Approach
Construct the SMB and HML factors from size and value corner portfolios, then regress each stock on market, size and value with Newey-West (HAC) standard errors.
What I built
A cached Streamlit dashboard with EWMA volatility, rolling-beta tracking, a factor heatmap and decomposition, and a bias-diagnostics module covering survivorship, look-ahead and sample attrition.

Limitations
The dashboard states its own caveat: it projects today’s Nifty 50 backward, so survivorship bias is present. Building the diagnostics made me much more careful about survivorship and look-ahead bias generally.

SpaceX DCF

Valuation · Financial modelling · Excel

GitHub Write-up on LinkedIn Model cover, PDF

How much is SpaceX actually worth, built up from its own S-1/A filing?

Source
SEC Form S-1/A, Amendment No. 2 (filed 3 June 2026). Valuation date 19 June 2026; SpaceX listed on Nasdaq on 12 June 2026 at $135 a share. Every hard-coded number is in blue font and traceable to the filing.
What I built
A full three-statement model with segment forecasts for Space, Starlink and AI, integrated operating schedules, Bear, Base and Bull scenarios toggled from one assumptions cell, and an interactive dashboard.
Gordon Growth
$10.77Base · Bear $0.68 · Bull $32.63
Exit multiple
$30.46Base · Bear $9.03 · Bull $63.15
Market price
$157.54Nasdaq close, 1 July 2026
Result
Every scenario, on both methods, came out well below the market price, mainly because a large part of the value ended up in terminal value.
Why
Years of negative free cash flow from launch infrastructure, satellites and AI compute kept the forecast-period cash flows small. It was a good reminder that a DCF is only as good as its assumptions, particularly growth, margins, capex, WACC and terminal value.

An educational valuation exercise, not investment advice.

Morning Dashboard & Research Terminal

Equity research · AI-assisted · Python

Overview on LinkedIn

Most people build market automations to get signals. I built mine to get better at thinking.

The morning dashboard
Refreshes before I wake up: India startup and VC activity, India macro (RBI, SEBI, GST, bonds, the rupee), global macro (Fed, ECB, China, yields, oil), a market snapshot, an IPO tracker and the day’s calendar. Then one concept explained properly, one deep dive, one interview question with the common mistake, and a pattern of the day that forces the stories into a single thesis.
The research terminal
Studies two to four companies every few days, each from a different industry and each picked because something happened: earnings, guidance, an acquisition, a regulatory decision. Nine sections per company, built on FMP market data, across semiconductors, medical devices, specialty retail and more.
No calls
No buy or sell calls anywhere; the output is a set of questions I have to answer myself. A source-tier check rejects unverified claims and flags discrepancies, so the system admits what it doesn’t know instead of confidently filling the gap.

Matiks Retention Case

Product case competition · 2026

Read the case

Which feature next, or is that entirely the wrong question?

Approach
A hand-assembled dataset of 947 app-store reviews, 130 community messages and eight search-volume exports, around one question: why doesn’t playing Matiks spread Matiks?
The finding
A 4.6M-view campaign converted 3.16K subscribers, and almost nobody searches for what the product sells. It can’t buy growth and can’t rank for it, so it has to grow itself. The brief moved from “which feature next” to “nothing a match produces compounds.”
Placement
Top 3.3%of 9,172 registrants
Deck
24slides
Spend gate
50player k-factor test
My answer
Make every match mint two things: a Challenge Card a friend has to install to answer, and a Cognitive Portfolio that makes leaving expensive. Then a gate: if the card’s k-factor doesn’t move toward 1 in the first quarter, fix the card before spending anything on growth.
Outcome
It made the case round; it didn’t make the top 10, and I posted the deck anyway.

RemitRight

Fintech · FX · Course: FinTech, Shiv Nadar University

Write-up

Remittance providers take 0.3–0.8% above the mid-market rate, and most people sending money home never see it.

The signal
Scores today’s USD/INR spread against a 30-day rolling baseline using z-scores on RBI DBIE data, and reports green, amber or red.
The calendar
A structural risk calendar flagging the predictable surges: month-end, FOMC and pre-Diwali.
The true cost
An all-in channel comparator that includes the correspondent-bank charges most apps leave out, plus alerts the moment the spread hits a target. On a $1,500 transfer the hidden spread is ₹600–1,200, every month.
Feedback
Our professor asked the question that reshaped the business: if remittance companies don’t partner with you, you’re just an information portal, so what’s the moat? Built with a team of six.
