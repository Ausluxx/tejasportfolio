NSE Momentum + Quality

Factor investing · Python · Streamlit

Live dashboard GitHub

Do momentum and quality still earn their keep on Indian large-caps once you pay real trading costs?

Approach
Rank stocks on combined momentum and quality signals, weighting the two by their measured information coefficient (IC²) over a 36-month training window rather than by gut feel.
What I built
A long-only strategy with portfolios built by a quadratic-programming optimiser under position limits, tested walk-forward with transaction costs, and an interactive dashboard to compare turnover settings.

Result
Out of sample from February 2022 to June 2026, net of costs, the chosen setting (λ = 0.05) compounded to 1.81× against 1.38× for the index. Best year +34.4% (2023), worst year (6.0%) (2026).
What it taught me
The most aggressive setting had the highest gross alpha but paid the most in costs; trading less won on a net basis. Momentum’s IC was borderline (−0.0018) against quality’s +0.0064, so the blend leaned 58% quality. Out-of-sample testing and costs are what separate an edge from a curve-fit.

Backtest results are hypothetical and net of modelled transaction costs.

Fama-French Factor Engine

Quantitative finance · Python · Streamlit

Live dashboard GitHub

What drives Indian equity returns beyond the market, and how do you report it without overstating the case?

Approach
Construct the SMB and HML factors from size and value corner portfolios, then regress each stock on market, size and value with Newey-West (HAC) standard errors.
What I built
A cached Streamlit dashboard with EWMA volatility, rolling-beta tracking, a factor heatmap and decomposition, and a bias-diagnostics module covering survivorship, look-ahead and sample attrition.

What it taught me
Why a single market beta was never enough, and the statistical discipline that keeps a factor result honest instead of merely impressive. The dashboard states its own caveat: it projects today’s Nifty 50 backward, so survivorship bias is present.

SpaceX DCF

Valuation · Financial modelling · Excel

GitHub Model cover, PDF

What is one of the most-watched private companies on earth actually worth, from first principles?

What I built
A full three-statement model feeding a discounted-cash-flow valuation, with scenario analysis and sensitivity tables.
The check
Terminal value cross-checked two ways, Gordon Growth against an exit multiple, because the terminal value usually carries most of a DCF.
What it taught me
DCF mechanics and the tyranny of terminal-value assumptions. A valuation is only as honest as its sensitivity table.

RemitRight

Fintech · FX · Course: FinTech, Shiv Nadar University

Write-up

People sending money home rarely know whether the rate is fair. Can a spread simply tell them?

The signal
Scores today’s USD/INR spread against a 30-day rolling baseline using z-scores on RBI DBIE data, and reports green, amber or red.
The calendar
A structural risk calendar flagging the predictable surges: month-end, FOMC and pre-Diwali.
The true cost
An all-in cost comparator that includes the correspondent-bank charges most remittance apps leave out.
What it taught me
Statistical baselines and mean reversion, how macro events move FX spreads, and why a transfer always costs more than its headline rate.
