### Project 1 — NSE Momentum + Quality Factor Backtest
*Quant equity strategy · Python · Live dashboard*

**The idea:** Do momentum and quality — two of the best-documented equity factors — still earn their keep on Indian large-caps once you account for real trading costs? This is my attempt to test that honestly rather than take it on faith.

**What it does:**
- A long-only strategy that ranks NSE stocks on combined momentum and quality signals, weighting the two by their information coefficient rather than guessing.
- Uses a quadratic-programming optimizer to build the portfolio under real-world constraints.
- **Walk-forward backtesting with transaction costs** — testing on data the model hasn't seen, so results aren't flattered by hindsight.
- An interactive dashboard to explore performance and holdings.

**The finance behind it:** factor investing, signal combination, portfolio construction under constraints, and why out-of-sample testing and costs are what separate a real edge from a curve-fit.

**Proof:**
- **Live dashboard →** https://nse-momentum-quality-prj2-nse50.streamlit.app/

### Project 2 — Fama-French 3-Factor Model (India)
*Factor model · Python · Live dashboard*

**The idea:** The Fama-French three-factor model is the standard lens for explaining stock returns beyond the market. I rebuilt it for Indian equities to see how market, size, and value factors behave here.

**What it does:**
- Estimates factor exposures for Indian equities across market, size (SMB), and value (HML).
- **Rolling betas** to show how those exposures drift over time rather than assuming they're fixed.
- **Newey-West (HAC) standard errors** so the statistics hold up when returns are autocorrelated and heteroskedastic.
- A multi-panel visualization dashboard.

**The finance behind it:** what drives cross-sectional returns, why a single market beta isn't enough, and how to report factor results without overstating their significance.

**Proof:**
- **Live dashboard →** https://factor-analytics-terminal-nse50.streamlit.app/
- **GitHub →** https://github.com/Ausluxx/factor-analytics-terminal
- *Screenshot: `FAMA 3 FACTOR.png`*

### Project 4 — RemitRight: USD/INR Spread Intelligence Platform
*Fintech · Course: FinTech, Shiv Nadar University*

**The idea:** People sending money across borders rarely know if they're getting a fair rate. RemitRight turns the USD/INR remittance spread into a simple GREEN / AMBER / RED signal.

**What it does:**
- Applies **z-score modelling on RBI DBIE data** to score today's spread against a 30-day rolling baseline.
- A **structural risk calendar** that flags predictable spread surges — month-end, FOMC dates, pre-Diwali demand.
- An **all-in cost comparator** that includes the correspondent-bank charges most remittance apps quietly leave out.

**The finance behind it:** statistical baselines and mean reversion, how macro events move FX spreads, and why the "true" cost of a transfer is bigger than the headline rate.
