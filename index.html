About

02About

Finance only really clicked when I left the classroom.

Three years in, the things that taught me most weren’t lectures. They were a merchant bank’s deal room in Gurugram, a student treasury with twenty lakh rupees and fifty-eight clubs depending on it, and a long run of evenings spent rebuilding factor models until the numbers finally behaved.

What I actually enjoy is the interrogation — why this multiple, why that spread, what happens to the story if the assumption is wrong. I’m upfront about the boundary too: I use modern tooling, AI included, to build these projects. The financial reasoning underneath each one is mine, and I’ll happily be questioned on it.

Right now I’m in my final years at Shiv Nadar University, serving as Vice President of the Student Council, and looking for the analyst seat where I get to do this properly.
