SpaceX DCF

Valuation · Three-statement model · Excel

How much is SpaceX actually worth, built up from its own S-1/A filing?

Approach
A full three-statement model built from the S-1/A (Amendment No. 2), with segment forecasts for Space, Starlink and AI, integrated operating schedules, and Bear, Base and Bull scenarios. Terminal value is cross-checked two ways: Gordon Growth and an exit multiple.

$10.77per share

$30.46per share

$63.15Bull, exit multiple
Market price
$157.54Nasdaq close, 1 July 2026
GitHub Write-up Full write-up

What it taught me. Even the most optimistic case sits far below the market. That doesn’t make the market wrong; it shows how much of SpaceX’s value rests on expectations beyond a five-year forecast. Years of negative free cash flow from launch, satellite and AI-compute spending pushed most of the value into the terminal value.

An educational valuation exercise, not investment advice.

NSE Momentum + Quality

Factor investing · Python · Live dashboard

Do momentum and quality still earn their keep on Indian large-caps once you pay real trading costs?

Approach
Rank Nifty 50 stocks on both signals and weight the signals by their measured information coefficient (IC²) rather than by gut feel. Build portfolios with a quadratic-programming optimiser under position limits, then test walk-forward with transaction costs.

What it taught me. The most aggressive setting had the highest gross alpha, but a moderate turnover penalty won once costs came out: trading less paid for itself. Momentum’s measured IC was borderline (−0.0018) against quality’s +0.0064, so the model leaned 58% quality.

Backtest results are hypothetical and net of modelled transaction costs.
